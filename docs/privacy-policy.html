Information We Collect

We collect the following information directly from you when you use the App:

WhenWhat we collect
Creating an accountFull name, email address, password, phone number (optional)
Signing in with GoogleYour name, email address, and profile photo, as provided by Google

How We Use Your Information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Third-Party Services

We share limited data with the following third-party services, solely to operate the App:

Your account and inquiry data is stored on our backend infrastructure, hosted via Railway.

Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal information is deleted from our systems, except where we are required to retain it for legal or security purposes.

Your Rights

You can review and update your profile information directly within the App. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, contact us using the details below.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including encrypting stored passwords and securing data in transit. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.
